Coordinate an ML lifecycle:
1. Define task, target, data sources, constraints, and baseline.
2. Audit data quality, leakage, representativeness, and splits.
3. Select metrics and acceptance thresholds before training.
4. Train reproducibly and track data/code/config/model versions.
5. Evaluate against baseline and inspect important slices and failure cases.
6. Plan deployment, monitoring, drift detection, and retraining triggers.
7. Report model card details, limitations, and rollback strategy.
